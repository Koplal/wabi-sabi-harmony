## PER-NEIGHBORHOOD FACT BRIEFS

### Oak Bay
- Detached median ~$1.8M (Apr 2026); highest assessed ~$17.3M; 2025 record non-waterfront $6.975M.
- 100+ registered heritage properties. Arts & Crafts, Tudor Revival, Colonial Revival, Craftsman.
- Architect **Samuel Maclure** (~500 homes; half-timbering set the tone).
- Landmarks: Bowker Gates (1913, Maclure), Royal Victoria Yacht Club (1912), Uplands Park (76-acre
  Garry Oak reserve), 400-year-old Garry oaks on Beach Drive, Chinese Cemetery at Harling Point (NHS).
- Materials: cast limestone fireplaces, wrought iron, marble, native wood, heritage windows.
- Concierge angle: oceanfront heritage estates; salt-air degradation of marble/limestone/windows;
  discretion for $17M+ properties.

### Uplands (Oak Bay)
- Avg list ~$6.36M; median ~$4.7M (range $2M–$28M); MLS record 3155 Beach Drive $13.195M (Nov 2023).
- **National Historic Site (2019)** — Olmsted-designed garden suburb (John Charles Olmsted, 1907).
- 465-acre neighbourhood, serpentine roads, Uplands Golf Course, Royal Victoria Yacht Club, private
  beach access, Olympic Mountain views. Riffington estate (1913, Philip A. Jullien, Tudor Revival).
- Materials: 500+ tonnes stone (sandstone/Indiana limestone), marble, heritage millwork, cast limestone.
- Concierge angle: nationally-historic trophy estates; heritage-preservation compliance; ongoing
  salt-air remediation; peak discretion.

### Rockland
- Historic "Nob Hill" (1890s–1910s Victorian/Edwardian). 100 properties on City Heritage Register.
- Two National Historic Sites: **Craigdarroch Castle** (1887–90, Dunsmuir, Scottish Baronial, 25,000+
  sq ft) and **Government House** (Lt. Governor's residence, 36-acre grounds, formal gardens).
- Architects Maclure & Rattenbury. Tudor Revival, Arts & Crafts, Classical/Colonial Revival, Italianate.
- Elevated, 5 min from downtown; Garry oak ecosystems; lush gardens.
- Materials: stone masonry, half-timbering, Victorian chimneys, heritage windows.
- Concierge angle: heritage-designation constraints + code compliance; specialist masonry-aware care.

### Fairfield
- SFH avg ~$1.6M (range $940K–$3.75M). "Most stable market in Victoria." Tree-lined early-1900s homes.
- Heritage: Ross Bay Villa (1865, Gothic Revival, John Wright) heritage museum; Arts & Crafts bungalows,
  Craftsman, Edwardian foursquares. Dallas Road oceanfront, Cook Street Village, Moss Street Market,
  Beacon Hill Park (200 acres), 20km waterfront walkway.
- Concierge angle: heritage/Craftsman finishes; Dallas Road salt-spray exposure; affluent walkable elegance.

### Cadboro Bay (Saanich)
- SFH avg ~$1.72M; 2025 avg listing ~$2.14M. Seaside village + heritage/character homes.
- **Queenswood Drive**: oceanfront homes with in-ground pools and private docks. Cadboro-Gyro Park
  (1.5km sandy beach, "Cadborosaurus" sculptures), UVic adjacent, old-growth trails.
- Concierge angle: oceanfront premium; salt-spray window/exterior care; Queenswood estate discretion.

### Ten Mile Point (Saanich)
- SFH avg ~$2.45M; SE pocket ~$3.35M; listings to $4.95M. Rocky peninsula into Haro Strait.
- Mid-century + custom contemporary oceanfront-bluff estates, floor-to-ceiling glazing, half-acre+ lots,
  private beach/deep-water moorage. Ten Mile Point Ecological Reserve; secluded coves; top diving.
- Materials: exposed local stone, Douglas fir, heated concrete, teak/Ipe decking.
- Concierge angle: extreme salt-spray zone; view-glass cleaning; moorage; ultra-privacy ($3–5M estates).

### Gordon Head (Saanich)
- Saanich East benchmark ~$1.18M (Mar 2025); premium lots $1.6–2.2M. 1950s–80s + renovated West Coast.
- UVic-adjacent; Mount Douglas Park (PKOLS, 182 ha summit views); Arbutus/Ferndale/Mayfair drives.
- Materials: engineered/old fir hardwood, heated concrete, quartz/stone, custom millwork, cedar.
- Concierge angle: material-rich renovated homes; dual-income professional/empty-nester demographic.

### Broadmead (Saanich)
- Master-planned executive enclave (late 1970s+). SFH ~$1.3–2.2M+, custom lots >$2.5M. Top-5 avg-price.
- Curvilinear cul-de-sacs; Broadmead Village; Rithet's Bog Conservation Area (50 ha); Elk/Beaver Lake.
- Materials: marble/granite, hardwood, stone foyers, cedar shakes, smart-home (Lutron/Crestron).
- Concierge angle: 3,000–5,000+ sq ft executive homes; Swartz Bay/YYJ commuters → absence management;
  word-of-mouth referral culture.

### James Bay (Victoria)
- **Oldest residential neighbourhood in BC** (1850s; ex-HBC Beckley Farm). 150+ heritage buildings.
- Helmcken House (c.1852, oldest surviving building), Woodlands (1861). Victorian/Edwardian, Italianate,
  Queen Anne. Houses median ~$1.6M; neighbourhood list median ~$870K.
- Waterfront: Dallas Road, Ogden Point breakwater, Fisherman's Wharf, Beacon Hill Park, Parliament.
- Luxury condos: Shoal Point (21 Dallas Rd, 24hr concierge, penthouse $8.9M), Harbourside ($2.8M PH).
- Concierge angle: heritage-home period finishes; lock-and-leave waterfront condos; pied-à-terre owners.

### Downtown Victoria
- Heritage conversions + luxury towers. Customs House (Empire-style federal bldg → luxury condos),
  Morley Soda Factory (1875, restored 2009), The Pearl Residences (1628 Store St, 133 units), The Falls.
- Condo median ~$551K (May 2026); range $255K studios → $2.6M+ penthouses; Pearl ~$935/sq ft.
- Inner Harbour, David Foster Harbour Pathway. Marble baths, granite/quartz, floor-to-ceiling glass.
- Concierge angle: lock-and-leave, pied-à-terre/corporate, heritage-conversion finishes, white-glove.

### Sidney (Sidney-by-the-Sea)
- Upscale walkable seaside town; "Retirement Capital of Canada", Canada's only Booktown. ~12,000 pop.
- SFH median ~$1.02M (Mar 2026); oceanfront condos. Coast Waterfront Residences (Lochside Dr, West Coast
  contemporary). 5–10 min to YYJ airport & Swartz Bay ferries; Salish Sea/Gulf Islands views.
- Concierge angle: affluent retirees, second-home owners, oceanfront condo salt-air care, social calendar.

### North Saanich (incl. Lands End Road, Deep Cove)
- North Saanich median ~$1.25M (50% premium vs Sidney); estates to $5M+; rural-residential acreage, ALR,
  equestrian, marinas (Canoe Cove 380 berths, Deep Cove, Van Isle).
- **Lands End Road**: signature luxury waterfront corridor on Haro Strait. 1850 Lands End $19.08M (#2
  most expensive on Vancouver Island, Jan 2026); 848 Lands End $12.6M (2024 build, 7,349 sq ft + guest
  house, Sub-Zero/Viking, Crestron, award-winning kitchen/bath, low-bank oceanfront, 350 sq ft deck).
- Materials: floor-to-ceiling glass, hardwood, natural stone, smart-home, Step-5 energy.
- Concierge angle: 7,000+ sq ft waterfront estates, guest houses, yacht/moorage, absentee owners, YYJ.

### Cordova Bay (Saanich)
- Saanich East benchmark ~$1.43M (Dec 2024); non-waterfront $1.5–2M; waterfront $3–5M+ (to $5–8M).
- Cordova Bay Road beachfront; Doumac/Haliburton estate streets; Mattick's Farm boutique village;
  Cordova Bay Golf Course; The Ridge enclave. Faces Haro Strait/San Juans.
- Materials: West Coast timber, cedar/fir, floor-to-ceiling glass, granite/slate, metal roofing, ipe decking.
- Concierge angle: vacation/secondary homes, salt-air glass/cedar care, absentee key-holding, event turnover.

### Willis Point (Central Saanich)
- Ultra-secluded (~150 homes) above Tod Inlet, across from Butchart Gardens. Single access road
  (Willis Point Road). Acreage 1–10+; timber-frame/post-and-beam, A-frame/lodge; inlet views.
- Central Saanich benchmark ~$1.29M (Dec 2024); acreage listings $1.8–3.5M; waterfront $4–5M+.
- Borders Gowlland Tod Provincial Park; Tod Creek. Materials: heavy fir/cedar timber, stone, reclaimed
  wood, metal roofing, radiant/geothermal.
- Concierge angle: EPITOME of "quiet luxury / discreet concierge"; single-road exclusivity; forest debris
  on cedar/timber; long vacancies need inspection; no competition reaches the enclave.

### Prospect Lake (Saanich West)
- Rare lakefront luxury. Saanich West benchmark ~$1.20M (Dec 2024); lakefront $2–4M+ (to $4.5–5M).
- Prospect Lake Rd / Munn Rd / Sparton Rd; 1.5km private-ish lake; Francis/King & Thetis Lake parks.
- Materials: cedar/fir, fieldstone fireplaces, wide-plank oak, triple-pane lake glazing, metal roofing.
- Concierge angle: lakefront pollen/algae/moisture on cedar/stone/hardwood; seasonal open/close; boat/dock.

### Dean Park Estates (North Saanich)
- Elevated (100–200m) executive estates beside John Dean Provincial Park (244m summit). North Saanich
  benchmark ~$1.63M (Dec 2024, highest peninsula benchmark); Dean Park $1.6–3.5M, view lots $4–6M.
- Dean Park Rd / Tatlow Rd / Blue Heron Rd; Patricia Bay, Saanich Inlet, Gulf Islands, Olympic views.
- Materials: timber/cedar, granite/limestone, large-format glazing, oak floors, heated driveways, smart-home.
- Concierge angle: executive commuters (ferry/airport) → absentee management; 4,000–7,000 sq ft; view-glass.
